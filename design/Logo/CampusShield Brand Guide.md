# CampusShield AI Logo and Brand Guide

## Brand Idea

CampusShield AI represents a visible layer of protection around the KNUST community. The identity should communicate vigilance, trust, campus belonging, and practical technology.

## Wordmark

**CampusShield** is the primary name. **AI** is the compact technology accent and should remain visually connected to the wordmark.

Recommended lockup:

```text
[shield mark] CampusShield AI
             KNUST Security Platform
```

## Shield Mark Direction

Use a simple geometric shield with a clear internal check or campus path motif. The mark must remain recognisable at small sizes and must not depend on fine detail. The existing interface uses a shield icon in the sidebar and should remain the reference for the prototype.

## Colour Palette

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Campus Navy | `#102A43` | Wordmark, navigation, headings |
| Signal Blue | `#1976D2` | Primary actions, links, active states |
| Campus Gold | `#FDB913` | AI accent, highlights, selected emphasis |
| Safety Red | `#C62828` | Emergency and high-risk status |
| Alert Amber | `#C88719` | Warnings and medium risk |
| Safe Green | `#2E7D5B` | Success and low risk |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Reversed logo and dark surfaces |

## Usage Rules

- Keep generous clear space around the mark equal to the shield width.
- Use the navy/gold lockup on light backgrounds.
- Use the white lockup on navy or dark backgrounds.
- Keep the AI accent gold and do not recolour it as a risk indicator.
- Never stretch, rotate, outline, or add drop shadows to the mark.
- Do not place the full logo over a busy map or photograph without a solid backing area.

## Minimum Sizes

- Digital full lockup: 140px wide minimum.
- Digital shield mark: 24px square minimum.
- Print full lockup: 35mm wide minimum.

## Presentation Asset Checklist

- Cover slide logo with transparent background.
- Dark-background reversed logo.
- Light-background primary logo.
- Favicon or app icon using the shield mark only.
- Consistent footer lockup for report pages.

## Accessibility

The logo should have alternative text such as `CampusShield AI logo`. Do not communicate system status through the gold accent alone; pair risk states with labels and accessible text.
